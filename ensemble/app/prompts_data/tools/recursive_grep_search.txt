Regex search, recursive, in a directory on the operator host, returning matching lines with paths and line numbers. Read-only and safe for automated use. Use it to find strings or patterns across many files.

<behavior>
- Filter files with the `includes` globs. Results are capped at `max_matches` (default 100, max 500).
- Skips hidden directories such as `.git`. Enforces symlink boundaries.
</behavior>
